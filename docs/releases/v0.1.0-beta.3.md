# MellowDesk v0.1.0-beta.3

Release date: 2026-08-12

Status: public beta

## Overview

This beta makes scheduled breaks difficult to miss. At the due time, MellowDesk now presents a persistent reminder anchored to its menu-bar leaf. It stays visible until the user starts the routine, snoozes for 10 minutes, or pauses reminders for the day.

## Added

- A persistent menu-bar reminder with Start, Snooze 10 minutes, and Pause Today actions.
- Durable overdue-reminder state that survives app restarts and is reconciled after wake, activation, clock changes, and time-zone changes.
- A system notification fallback when the app-owned reminder cannot appear exactly at the due time.

## Fixed

- The reminder no longer depends on a five-second macOS banner being noticed.
- Starting from the nonactivating reminder card reliably brings the workout window to the front without leaving a hidden session behind the current app.
- Unique notification request identifiers prevent scheduling the next cycle from replacing the prior delivered reminder.
- Ordinary settings changes no longer dismiss an unresolved reminder.
- Snooze and pause no longer restart reminders over an active workout.
- Stale notification actions from older builds are ignored.
- Reminder actions remain enabled on later cycles without requiring an app restart.
- Concurrent refreshes for the same due time no longer remove the current system-notification fallback.

## Download

The GitHub Release provides a universal `arm64 + x86_64` macOS archive and its SHA-256 checksum. The app is signed by EigenLogic with Developer ID, uses Hardened Runtime, is notarized by Apple, and includes a stapled notarization ticket.

## Validation

- 45 deterministic automated tests pass.
- CI validates Apple Silicon and Intel runners.
- On a real Mac, the reminder remained visible while switching apps and closed only after an explicit action.
- With Finder in front and MellowDesk inactive, Start closed the reminder and brought a single fresh workout window to the foreground.
- The previously accepted camera calibration, rotation, lateral-flexion, gentle-nod, completion-history, single-user, and camera-shutdown checks remain valid because this release does not change those paths.

## Known limitations

- The app UI is Chinese.
- Camera counting is approximate and depends on lighting, framing, camera model, and individual movement.
- Manual counting may be needed when the camera is unavailable or recognition is unreliable.
- The release does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized rehabilitation.

## Privacy and feedback

Camera frames and pose samples remain local and are not recorded, saved, or uploaded. Read [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) for the full data inventory and deletion controls.

Before reporting a bug, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Do not attach faces, camera recordings, raw pose data, or personal workout history to a public issue. Report vulnerabilities privately using [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
