# MellowDesk v0.1.0-beta.2

Release date: 2026-08-12

Status: public beta

## Overview

This maintenance beta fixes the live “next reminder” countdown in the menu-bar panel. In beta.1, the displayed minute could remain frozen while the panel stayed open even though the scheduled reminder time itself was unchanged and still valid.

## Fixed

- The relative countdown now owns a SwiftUI clock and updates as time passes.
- Paused and unscheduled reminder states keep their existing wording and behavior.
- Reminder cadence, work-hour settings, workout history, camera processing, and stored user data are unchanged.

## Download

The GitHub Release provides a universal `arm64 + x86_64` macOS archive and its SHA-256 checksum. The app is signed by EigenLogic with Developer ID, uses Hardened Runtime, is notarized by Apple, and includes a stapled notarization ticket.

## Validation

- Automated checks pass on Apple Silicon and Intel GitHub Actions runners.
- The countdown was verified on a real Mac while the menu remained open across a minute boundary.
- The existing real-camera acceptance results for calibration, all three movements, completion history, single-user face handling, and camera shutdown remain valid because this release does not change those paths.

## Known limitations

- The app UI is Chinese.
- Camera counting is approximate and depends on lighting, framing, camera model, and individual movement.
- Manual counting may be needed when the camera is unavailable or recognition is unreliable.
- The release does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized rehabilitation.

## Privacy and feedback

Camera frames and pose samples remain local and are not recorded, saved, or uploaded. Read [PRIVACY.md](../../PRIVACY.md) for the full data inventory and deletion controls.

Before reporting a bug, read [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Do not attach faces, camera recordings, raw pose data, or personal workout history to a public issue. Report vulnerabilities privately using [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).
